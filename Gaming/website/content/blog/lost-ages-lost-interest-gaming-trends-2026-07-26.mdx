## The Fading Lure of Hardware Specs

In a recent interview, Shigeru Miyamoto, the iconic creator of Mario, expressed his thoughts on the changing gaming landscape. The evidence is clear: gamers are no longer fixated on the latest graphics cards, CPU cores, or RAM capacities. Instead, they're drawn to immersive experiences, engaging narratives, and seamless gameplay.

This shift in focus is reflected in the latest trends in the gaming industry. The rise of cloud gaming, game streaming, and subscription-based services has led to a decrease in the emphasis on hardware specifications. Gamers are no longer concerned with the intricacies of GPU architecture or the performance of the latest CPU.
